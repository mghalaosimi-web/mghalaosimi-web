# M.GH.AL — Profile photo library

This folder is reserved for the owner's approved public profile photographs.

## Upload your photographs

1. Open the upload page for this folder:
   https://github.com/mghalaosimi-web/mghalaosimi-web/upload/content%2Fprofile-photo-library-2026-11/public/images/profile
2. Sign in to the GitHub account that has write access to this repository.
3. Select or drag your original photographs into the upload area.
4. Use descriptive filenames (Latin characters, lowercase, hyphens), for example:
   - `mohammed-ghaleb-alaosimi-profile.jpg`
   - `mghal-professional-work.jpg`
   - `mghal-speaking-event.jpg`
5. Add a commit message such as `Add approved profile photographs`, then choose **Commit changes** on this branch.

## Important privacy and publishing notes

- This repository is public. Any photograph committed here becomes publicly accessible; upload only photographs you explicitly approve for public release.
- Do not upload identity documents, private/family photographs, location metadata you do not want public, or images of other people without their permission.
- Prefer JPG, PNG, or WebP. Keep original quality; avoid very large files (ideally under 5 MB per image).
- Uploading files here does not automatically publish them on the live website. The images must first be reviewed, referenced by the site, and merged through a pull request.
- Keep the original filenames stable after the website begins referencing them.
